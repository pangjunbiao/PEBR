matched-amount sharing and the same-score similarity-prefix control in all
five displayed settings on BOTH collections: the original reference/fold
setting, two reference initializations and two alternative family-fold
assignments. These are paired five-final-seed means within each setting,
not five independent corpora and not a significance claim.
