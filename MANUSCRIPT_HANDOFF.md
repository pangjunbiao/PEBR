# What the manuscript must describe after final result inspection

1. PEBR is the frozen receiver-preserving, fixed-cap forward rule. Its
   score rewards hidden-word predictive gain and charges visible-word losses,
   weighted by the corresponding receiver token shares. The downside utility
   then penalizes negative view scores. Remove the old positive-weight-grid
   search description: every positive offered weight is the fixed cap.
2. Keep no borrowing as an explicit admissible action. Distinguish selected
   donors from donors available at final transfer, offered cap from realized
   mass, and an empty action from availability fallback. Unused shares stay
   with the receiver. Topic memberships use original observed words with the
   final learned dictionary fixed.
3. State theoretical guarantees on the scored selection views and normalized
   count distributions. They do not imply improved protected words for every
   post, improved final coherence, semantic issue separation, or a count-mass
   bound after an arbitrary TF-IDF transformation. Forward search is not a
   claim of globally optimal subset selection.
4. Separate five final-fit seeds from four fresh masking banks, alternative
   reference initializations, family-fold assignments and protected outer
   holdouts. Report the unit of each SD. Reused fits do not become additional
   independent experiments. K12 is the fixed main revised-study count; K8/K10
